Words it understands
  • What to track: lessons tasks budget inventory books workouts volunteer hours sales, or anything ("track my plants")
  • Goal: goal of 600 minutes $8,000 budget 12 books 40 hours
  • People: for Ana, Ben, and Cara gives each a tab plus a leaderboard. my 20-person team adds a Person column.
  • Stars: 5 stars milestones no stars
  • Columns: due dates priority ratings notes links categories status
  • Tabs: dashboard log instructions, or turn them off with without dashboard
  • Look: green blue pink orange purple gray, any emoji, called "Title"
